# Pre-submission checklist

Report each item as pass, fail (with location in the candidate's numbering and fix) or not applicable.

## Tailoring
- [ ] Structure, headings and proportions match what the candidate or institution prescribed, unchanged by the skill
- [ ] Function map complete: every function located in a named chapter
- [ ] Warned deviations recorded with the candidate's decision

## Contribution and argument
- [ ] Contribution claim matches the degree level
- [ ] Contribution in the abstract and in a dedicated before/after section; no unsupported "first" claims
- [ ] Every research question or task traceable to findings, discussion and conclusion
- [ ] Introduction promises exactly what the conclusion delivers; both rewritten last
- [ ] One-page argument matches the assembled thesis

## Chapters
- [ ] Review built from a synthesis matrix; convergence, divergence and critique present; ends in gap and framework
- [ ] Every major method choice justified against alternatives; instruments in appendices
- [ ] Results organised by research question; each result in one format chosen by its message
- [ ] Each question answered in the discussion against the reviewed literature
- [ ] Recommendations (if the model requires them) traced to findings and justified
- [ ] Limitations specific; future work concrete
- [ ] Abstract states findings and contribution

## By publication (if applicable)
- [ ] Overarching introduction and integrative discussion
- [ ] Linking section before each paper: status, candidate contribution, role in the argument
- [ ] Co-author contribution statements
- [ ] Repetition across paper chapters minimised or cross-referenced

## Integrity and compliance
- [ ] Every reference verified and present in the list
- [ ] Similarity check run; self-reuse cited and permitted
- [ ] AI-use declaration completed per institutional template
- [ ] Ethics approvals, data management and deposit requirements met
- [ ] Format, word limit, front matter and declarations per regulations

## Style and presentation
- [ ] One citation style across chapters and included papers (unless regulations allow otherwise)
- [ ] Tables and figures numbered by chapter, called out, listed in front matter with matching titles
- [ ] Caption positions and label formats match the style
- [ ] Terminology and abbreviations consistent across chapters

## Finish
- [ ] Full proofread of the assembled document
- [ ] Supervisor has read the complete final draft
- [ ] Examiner suggestions and exclusions discussed with the supervisor where allowed
