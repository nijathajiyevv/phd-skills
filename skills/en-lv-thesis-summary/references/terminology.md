# Terminology: digitalization, SMEs, research methods

Two rules govern term selection.

**Rule 1 . standardized term wins.** Latvian has an official terminology apparatus: the Terminology Commission of the Latvian Academy of Sciences (LZA Terminoloģijas komisija) and the national term database at `termini.gov.lv` / AkadTerm. Where a term is standardized there, use that form. Where the field uses a competing form in practice, use the standardized form and note the variant in the glossary rather than switching mid-document.

**Rule 2 . consistency beats elegance.** One English term maps to one Latvian term for the whole document unless the source itself distinguishes senses. Record every mapping in `glossary.md` and never vary it for stylistic relief.

When a term is absent from this file: check the term database, check how the term is used in recent Latvian dissertations and in Latvian-language journals in the field, and if it remains unsettled, present the candidates to the author with the trade-offs rather than choosing silently.

## Terms that are commonly mistranslated

- **performance**. context-dependent. `sniegums` (achievement), `darbības rezultāti` (business results), `veiktspēja` (technical capacity). Never default to one.
- **management**. `vadība` (the activity/leadership), `pārvaldība` (governance/administration), `vadītāji` (the people). Choose by sense.
- **development**. `attīstība` (growth/evolution) vs `izstrāde` (creating something). A "model development" section is `modeļa izstrāde`, not `modeļa attīstība`.
- **implementation**. `ieviešana` (introducing into practice) vs `īstenošana` (carrying out). Policy is `īstenota`; technology is `ieviesta`.
- **assessment / evaluation**. `novērtējums` (the result), `novērtēšana` (the process), `izvērtējums` (appraisal).
- **impact / effect / influence**. `ietekme` covers most cases; `efekts` only in fixed technical collocations.
- **framework**. see § Terms above; do not translate every instance as `ietvars`.
- **policy**. `politika` (the policy) vs `nostādnes`/`vadlīnijas` (guidelines). Note that `politika` also means politics . disambiguate where confusion is possible.
- **enterprise**. `uzņēmums` in the SME sense; `uzņēmējdarbība` when it means the activity; `uzņēmība` when it means the personal quality.
- **survey**. `aptauja` (asking people) vs `apsekojums` (statistical survey of units, e.g. GUS enterprise surveys). Official statistics use `apsekojums`.
