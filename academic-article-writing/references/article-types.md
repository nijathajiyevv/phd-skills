# Article types and field variants

The venue's own list of article types and limits overrides this file. Identify the type first; never apply IMRaD rules to a conceptual paper or PRISMA rules to a narrative review.

## Types

| Type | Contribution | Typical structure | Non-negotiables | Reporting guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original research, quantitative | New empirical evidence | IMRaD | Aims or hypotheses, sample size rationale, effect sizes with uncertainty, pre-specified vs exploratory analyses | CONSORT, STROBE, venue checklist |
| Original research, qualitative | New understanding of meaning, process, experience | IMRaD variant | Researcher position, sampling logic, named analytic approach, quotes as evidence, trustworthiness | SRQR, COREQ |
| Mixed methods | Combined explanation | IMRaD plus integration | Where and how strands integrate; neither strand superficial | Venue norms |
| Systematic review, meta-analysis | Exhaustive reproducible synthesis | Protocol, search, screening, appraisal, synthesis | Registered protocol, full search strings, flow diagram, risk-of-bias appraisal | PRISMA |
| Scoping or mapping review | Map of what exists and gaps | As systematic, usually without pooling | Explicit scope question, charting method | PRISMA extensions |
| Narrative, critical, state-of-the-art review | Interpretive synthesis, framework, agenda | Thematic sections | Argument rather than inventory; explicit selection logic | None formal |
| Conceptual or theoretical | New framework, model, propositions | Argument-driven | Defined constructs, logical derivation, testable propositions, boundary conditions | None formal |
| Short communication | Urgent, significant finding | Compressed IMRaD | High impact, not incremental | As original research |
| Case report | Unusual case with a lesson | Case narrative | Consent; why the case matters | CARE |
| Perspective, commentary | Informed position | Essay | Opinion permitted, clearly argued | None |
| Conference paper (CS, engineering) | Contribution under a page limit | Introduction, related work, method, experiments, conclusion | Contribution list, baselines, ablations, venue checklist | NeurIPS, ICML, ACL checklists |

Review typology is not standardised: Grant and Booth (2009) described 14 review types (critical, literature, mapping, meta-analysis, mixed studies, overview, qualitative systematic, rapid, scoping, state-of-the-art, systematic, systematic search and review, systematized, umbrella) and found few with explicit, mutually exclusive methods. Ask the user to name the review type and its method.

**A summary of papers is not a publishable review.** A review earns publication through synthesis: a framework, a resolved contradiction, a map of evidence strength, or a research agenda. Many publishers bar unpublished original data in review articles.

## Field variants to recognise, not convert

| Field or venue style | Typical sequence | Field expectation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medicine, health, life sciences | IMRaD, often mandated | Reporting guideline compliance |
| Management and business journals | Introduction; theory and hypothesis development; method; results; discussion with theoretical and managerial implications | Explicit theoretical contribution plus practical implications |
| Engineering, computer science | Introduction; related work; proposed method or system; experiments; results; discussion; conclusion | Baselines, ablations, reproducibility statement, venue checklist |
| Humanities | Argument-led sections with descriptive headings | Thesis statement; engagement with counter-positions |
| Systematic reviews in any field | PRISMA-shaped methods and results | Reproducible search and appraisal |
| Conceptual papers | Argument and propositions | Constructs defined; boundary conditions |

## When nothing is prescribed

Offer two or three structures from the rows that fit the user's type and field, each with a one-line trade-off, and let the user choose.
