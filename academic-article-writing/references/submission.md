# Submission: packaging and venue legitimacy

Many desk rejections are checklist failures rather than judgements of the science. They are the cheapest to prevent.

## Pre-submission compliance pass

Take the venue's author guidelines as input and produce a pass/fail table:

```
| Requirement (from guide) | Draft status | Pass/Fail | Fix |
```

Cover at least: word or page limit and what counts toward it; required sections and order; abstract type and length; keywords; reference style; figure format, resolution and count; table limits; anonymisation for double-blind review (text, acknowledgements, file metadata, self-identifying citations); required statements (ethics, funding, conflicts, data availability, AI use, author contributions); supplementary material rules; preprint policy.

## Cover letter (3 to 5 short paragraphs)

1. The contribution in one sentence and the article type.
2. Why it fits this venue's readership and scope.
3. What is new versus the closest prior work.
4. Confirmation of originality, no parallel submission, required approvals.
5. Suggested or excluded reviewers with reasons, if the venue allows.

Tailor to the venue; never recycle a letter addressed to another journal.

## Venue legitimacy (Think. Check. Submit.)

Check before submitting: the publisher is identifiable and contactable; the journal appears in the ISSN Portal; claimed indexing can be confirmed; fees are stated clearly; the peer-review process is described; membership of COPE, DOAJ or OASPA can be verified. Submit only if most answers are yes.

Red flags: unsolicited emails promising fast or guaranteed acceptance, fake or unverifiable metrics, a name imitating an established journal, hidden fees. Blacklists alone are unreliable (reactive, false positives against small legitimate journals).

## Other rules

- No simultaneous submission to two venues.
- Check the venue's preprint policy before posting; norms differ by field and venue.
- Keep a ranked list of three venues; after rejection, fix what reviews exposed before resubmitting elsewhere (the same reviewer may be drawn again).
- Appeal a desk rejection only to correct a factual error.
