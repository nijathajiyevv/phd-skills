# Ethics: authorship, integrity, AI use

## Fabricated references: the specific risk of AI assistance

Studies of LLM-generated citations report high fabrication rates: 55% of GPT-3.5 and 18% of GPT-4 citations entirely fabricated in one multidisciplinary study (Walters & Wilder 2023), and an audit of 17,842 ACL, NAACL and EMNLP papers found papers with hallucinated references rising from 0.28% in 2024 to 2.59% in 2025. Fabricated citations typically combine real journal names and real authors with nonexistent titles, and often carry plausible but wrong DOIs.

Rules for the skill:

- Never generate a reference from memory as if verified.
- Mark every reference not verified against a database, DOI resolver or the user's files as UNVERIFIED.
- Check that a verified reference actually supports the claim it is attached to.
- Never fill missing metadata by guessing.

## Authorship (ICMJE)

An author meets all four criteria: substantial contribution to conception, design, data acquisition, analysis or interpretation; drafting or critical revision; final approval; accountability for the whole work. Funding acquisition, general supervision and language editing alone do not qualify; such contributors are acknowledged. Agree authorship and order early and revisit as contributions change. No gift, honorary or ghost authors.

## AI use (COPE, ICMJE)

AI tools cannot be authors because they cannot take responsibility. Authors disclose which tool was used and how: writing assistance in the acknowledgements, AI used in data collection, analysis or figures in the methods. Humans remain responsible for every word, image and citation. Publisher rules vary; some prohibit AI-generated or AI-altered images.

Disclosure template (adapt to the venue's wording):

```
During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [tool, version] for [purpose: language editing / structuring feedback / code assistance]. After using this tool, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.
```

## Other integrity rules

- No salami slicing; no republication of the same data without cross-reference.
- No self-plagiarism of text or figures without citation and permission.
- No coerced or padded citations.
- Do not paste unpublished manuscripts or confidential reviews into public AI tools.
- Declare conflicts of interest, funding and prior dissemination (preprints, theses, conference abstracts).

## Refusals

Refuse to fabricate data, results, quotes or references; to write content designed to evade plagiarism or AI detection; or to omit a required disclosure. Explain briefly and offer the legitimate alternative.
